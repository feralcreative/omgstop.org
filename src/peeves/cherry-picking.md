---
title: "cherry picking"
category: Logical fallacies
order: 114
summary: "You found the data that agreed with you and stopped looking."
rule: "Selecting the evidence that fits is not evidence. It's <em>editing</em>."
snark: "Now go read the other nine."
examples:
  - wrong: "\"Three reviews say it's great.\""
    right: "\"What do all the reviews say?\""
    note: "Three positives out of four hundred is a very different claim."
---

Every dataset large enough contains a subset supporting almost anything. Picking that subset feels like research and produces the opposite of it.

**How to answer it:** ask what the full picture looks like, and what was excluded. The excluded material is usually where the answer is.
