## The test

Rephrase the sentence as an answer using **he** or **him**.

- *He* did it → **who**
- Send it to *him* → **whom**

**Him** and **who**m both end in **M**. That's the whole mnemonic.

## Should you bother

*Whom* is fading, and in speech "who should I send it to" is completely normal. Nobody sounds wrong saying it.

Two places it still matters: formal writing, and directly after a preposition—*to whom*, *for whom*, *with whom*. *To who it may concern* is the version that gets noticed.

Getting it wrong in the other direction is worse. **Whom** deployed where **who** belongs, to sound careful, is the one that lands badly.
