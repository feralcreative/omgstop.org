## The mnemonic

A princip**le** is a ru**le**. Both end in **-le**, and that's the only one you need to remember—everything else is **principal**.

The old school mnemonic works too: the princi**pal** is your **pal**. Same spelling covers the main thing (*principal reason*) and the money in a loan (*principal and interest*).

## The one that gets noticed

*A matter of principal* is the common failure, and it reads as a spellcheck-clean typo in exactly the kind of sentence where you were trying to sound serious.
