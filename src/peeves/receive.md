## The trick

**I before E, except after C.** The C is right there. This is the textbook case.


The rule has famous exceptions—*weird*, *seize*, *their*, *caffeine*—which has made people distrust it entirely. For words with a *see* sound after a C (*receive*, *deceive*, *ceiling*, *perceive*), it holds every time.
