## The mnemonic

Compl**e**ment compl**e**tes. Both have an **E** in the middle, and one is the definition of the other.

Compl**i**ment is the nice one—**I** like it.

## Where it shows up

*Complement* turns up constantly in design and engineering writing—complementary colours, a complementary skill set, a full complement of staff. All of those mean "completes the set," and all of them get spelled with an I by people paying a compliment they didn't intend.
