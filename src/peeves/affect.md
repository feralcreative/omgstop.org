## The mnemonic

**A**ffect is an **A**ction. **E**ffect is the **E**nd result.

Or: if you can put *the* in front of it, you want **effect**. *The effect.* You cannot say *the affect* in ordinary usage.

## The exceptions nobody needs but everyone cites

**Effect** can be a verb meaning to bring about: *to effect change*. It's formal, it's rare, and it means something different from *affect change*—which isn't a phrase.

**Affect** can be a noun in psychology, meaning observable emotion: *flat affect*. If you're not writing a clinical note, this isn't you.

Both exist. Neither is what you meant. Use the mnemonic.
