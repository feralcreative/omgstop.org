## The words

**Bear** means to carry or endure—*bear the weight*, *bear the cost*, *bear with me* (endure me a moment).

**Bare** means uncovered, or to uncover.

## Why it matters more than the others

Most eggcorns are harmless. This one produces a sentence that means something specific, unfortunate, and easy to screenshot—which is why it's the one people remember seeing you write.
