---
title: "hasty generalization"
category: Logical fallacies
order: 112
summary: "You met three of them. There are eight billion people."
rule: "A small or lopsided sample tells you about the <em>sample</em>."
snark: "Two data points is not a trend."
examples:
  - wrong: "\"Both contractors we hired were unreliable, so contractors are unreliable.\""
    right: "\"How did we pick those two?\""
    note: "Two is not a sample. And how they were chosen may explain everything."
---

The problem is size and selection. A tiny sample is noisy, and a biased one is worse—it can be huge and still wrong, because how it was gathered decided the answer in advance.

**How to answer it:** ask how many, and how they were selected. If both answers are vague, the generalization is a vibe.
