---
title: "post hoc"
category: Logical fallacies
order: 110
summary: "It happened afterwards. That does not mean it happened because of."
rule: "\"After\" is not \"because.\""
snark: "The rooster does not cause the sunrise."
examples:
  - wrong: "\"Sales rose after the redesign, so the redesign worked.\""
    right: "\"What else changed that month?\""
    note: "Seasonality, a competitor folding, a press mention. Sequence isn't cause."
---

*Post hoc ergo propter hoc*—"after this, therefore because of this." Our pattern-matching is aggressive and does this automatically, which is why it takes deliberate effort to notice.

**How to answer it:** ask what else changed, and what would have happened with no intervention at all. If nobody can say, the causal claim is a guess with a timestamp.
