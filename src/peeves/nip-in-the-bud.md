## Where it comes from

Straight horticulture, in English since at least the 1600s. You nip off a **bud** before it opens, and the flower never forms. Applied to a problem: stop it while it's small.

**Butt** produces an image that is memorable, violent, and unrelated to stopping anything early.

This is an eggcorn—a misheard phrase reshaped into words you know. It's a reasonable mistake to make out loud and an unmissable one in writing.
