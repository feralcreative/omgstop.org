## Why it's wrong

**Regardless** = *regard* + *-less* = without regard. The suffix already does the negating.

Bolting **ir-** on the front adds a second negative, so the word technically means *not without regard*—the opposite of what anyone using it intends. It's a double negative wearing a suit.

## The annoying part

It's in the dictionary. Merriam-Webster lists it, marked *nonstandard*, because it's been recorded since the early 1900s and dictionaries record what people say.

Being in the dictionary is not the same as being usable in professional writing. *Nonstandard* is a warning label. **Regardless** is one syllable shorter and nobody has ever objected to it.
