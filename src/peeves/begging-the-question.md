Circular reasoning: the argument's support is the claim itself, restated. It sounds like reasoning because it has the shape of reasoning, but nothing enters that wasn't already assumed.

## The bonus peeve

**"Begs the question" does not mean "raises the question."** That's the far more common misuse and it's what most people saying it actually mean. If a fact makes you want to ask something, it *raises* a question. Begging the question is this specific logical error, and using it the other way makes the actual term unusable.

That ship may have sailed. It's still wrong.
