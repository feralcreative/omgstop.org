title: "the fallacy fallacy"
category: Logical fallacies
order: 126
summary: "Their argument was bad. Their conclusion might still be right."
rule: "A broken argument for a claim doesn't make the claim <em>false</em>."
snark: "You won the argument. That's all you won."
examples:
  - wrong: "\"You used a straw man, so you're wrong about the deadline.\""
    right: "\"Your reasoning was bad. Is the deadline still slipping?\""
    note: "Bad arguments and false conclusions are different failures."
---

Spotting a fallacy tells you the argument doesn't establish the conclusion. It tells you nothing about whether the conclusion is true—that still needs checking separately.

This one matters most on a page like this. Knowing the names makes it tempting to treat naming one as a win. It isn't. It just means that particular argument didn't work.
