## The three of them

- **Pique**—to provoke or arouse. From French *piquer*, to prick or sting. This is the one you want.
- **Peak**—the top of something. *Peaked* means it has crested and is now on the way down, which is a strange thing to say about your interest.
- **Peek**—a quick look.

## Bonus

*Pique* also means irritation—*he left in a fit of pique*. Same word, same root: something pricked at you.
