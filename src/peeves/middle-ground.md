Also the argument to moderation. Compromise is a fine way to settle preferences and a terrible way to settle facts. Where the middle lands depends entirely on how extreme the endpoints were, which means anyone can move it by staking out a sillier position.

**The legitimate version:** when both sides hold real evidence, the answer often *is* somewhere between. That's a conclusion you reach by weighing, not by averaging.
