## The trick

The word **finite** is sitting in the middle of it: de-**finite**-ly. *Finite* means having limits, and it has no A. Neither does this.


The A creeps in because the word is said fast and the middle vowel is unstressed, so your ear supplies a generic one. Spell it from *finite* outward and it can't happen.
