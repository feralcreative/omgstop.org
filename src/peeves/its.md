## Why this one is backwards

Every instinct you have says apostrophe-s means possession. For *its*, it doesn't—and that's the whole problem.

**Possessive pronouns never take an apostrophe.** Not *his*, *hers*, *theirs*, *ours*, or *yours*. Nobody writes *hi's*. *Its* is in that family and follows that rule.

The apostrophe version is only ever the contraction: **it is** or **it has**.

## The test

Say "it is." If the sentence holds, use **it's**. If it doesn't, use **its**. That is the entire rule, and it has zero exceptions.
