## The trick

**It is two Swedish words.** *Smörgås* is a sandwich, *bord* is a table. A sandwich table, which is exactly what the thing is.

*Smorgasboard* is the forgivable one. *Bord* and *board* are genuinely cognate, and English boards have been tables since long before anyone put a buffet on one. The instinct is right and the spelling is still wrong, because English borrowed the word whole.

*Smorgasborg* has no such excuse. *Borg* is Swedish for a castle or fortress—it is the back half of Göteborg and Helsingborg. You are describing a sandwich fortress.

Which, to be fair, is a better idea than the buffet.
