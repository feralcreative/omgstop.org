## The phrase

**Per se** is Latin: *per* (by, through) + *se* (itself). It means "in itself" or "intrinsically"—*the design isn't bad per se, it's just wrong for this*.

*Per say* isn't anything. It's what the phrase sounds like when you've only ever heard it.

Since it's a foreign phrase, some style guides italicise it. Nobody will mind either way. They will mind *per say*.
