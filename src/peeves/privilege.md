## The trick

**No D, and the vowels go i-i-e.** PRIV-I-LEGE.


The phantom D arrives from *knowledge* and *acknowledge*, which do have one. The *-lege* ending here comes from Latin *lex*, law—the same root as *legal*, which also has no D.
