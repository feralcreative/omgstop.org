## The sound test

**Loose** rhymes with *goose*—a hard S. **Lose** rhymes with *booze*—a Z sound.

Say the sentence out loud. If you hear a Z, one O. If you hear an S, two.

## Why it happens

English is inconsistent here and your instinct is reasonable: *choose* and *goose* look alike and sound different. There's no rule to derive, only the pair to memorise. One O for losing things, two for things that wobble.
