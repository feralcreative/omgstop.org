---
title: "straw man"
category: Logical fallacies
order: 100
summary: "You beat an argument nobody made. Impressive, irrelevant."
rule: "Refute what they <em>said</em>, not the dumbest thing you can imagine them saying."
snark: "So what you are saying is that nobody should ever speak again."
examples:
  - wrong: "\"We should test this before shipping.\" \"So you want to delay it forever?\""
    right: "\"How long would testing take?\""
    note: "Nobody said forever. A weaker version was invented because it's easier to beat."
---

The move is to swap someone's actual position for a flimsier one, knock that down, and act like you've won. It works because the audience often can't remember exactly what was claimed thirty seconds ago.

**How to answer it:** restate your position and ask them to address that one. If they can't do it without the exaggeration, the exaggeration was doing all the work.
