aliases:
  - whos
examples:
  - wrong: "Who's turn is it?"
    right: "Whose turn is it?"
    note: "The turn belongs to someone. Possessive, so *whose*."
  - wrong: "Whose bringing lunch?"
    right: "Who's bringing lunch?"
    note: "\"Who is bringing lunch.\" Contraction, so *who's*."
---

## The test

Say **"who is."** If it works, use **who's**. If it doesn't, use **whose**.

This is structurally identical to [its/it's](/its) and [your/you're](/your): apostrophe means a contraction, never possession. Possessive pronouns don't take apostrophes, and *whose* is one.

Learn one of these three and you've learned all three.
