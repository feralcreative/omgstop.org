## The test

Put a number in front of it. If it survives, it's **fewer**.

Three emails, three bugs, three people. Three waters, three traffics, three patiences. One of those lists is fine and one of them is nonsense, and now you know which is which.

That's the whole fucking rule.

## Before you argue

Yes, there are exceptions. No, none of them cover you.

- **Time, money, distance, weight** take *less*. Less than two hours. Less than $500. Less than five miles.
- ***One less thing***, never *one fewer thing*. Nobody talks like that.
- **Percentages** take *less*. *Less than 40 percent* is a proportion, not a pile.
- **Bare numbers** take *less*. *Less than 20* is fine. *Less than 20 **people*** is what we're here about.

Same split for **amount** and **number**: amount of water, number of bottles.

## In fairness

The rule is younger than it acts. Some guy in 1770 wrote that "fewer" struck him as *more elegant*, and everyone downstream has treated one man's preference as scripture ever since.

So no, "10 items or less" isn't a crime. It's just wrong in a way that a specific kind of asshole cannot stop noticing, and you are now on a first-name basis with one.
