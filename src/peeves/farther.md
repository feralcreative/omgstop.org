## The mnemonic

**Far**ther is about how **far**—actual, physical, measurable distance.

**Further** is for everything abstract: further discussion, further notice, further along in the process. It's also a verb—*to further your career*.

## The honest caveat

In British usage *further* covers both, and plenty of careful American writers use it for distance too. Nobody will misunderstand you.

What does get noticed is the other direction: **farther** used for something non-physical. *Farther discussion* has no defence. If you only remember one thing, remember that *farther* needs a distance.
