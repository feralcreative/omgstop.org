## The mnemonic

In **discrete**, the two Es are **separated** by the T—and *discrete* means separated.

In **discreet**, the Es huddle together, keeping quiet, which is what being discreet is.

It's a stupid mnemonic and it works permanently, which is the only test that matters.

## Where it bites

Technical writing is full of *discrete values*, *discrete events*, *discrete components*—all correctly spelled with the split Es. Spellcheck accepts both, so the wrong one ships silently into documentation and stays there.
