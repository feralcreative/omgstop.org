## The trick

**It's *re* + *commend*.** *Commend* has one C and two Ms, and adding the prefix changes nothing.


Splitting it at the prefix works for a lot of these: *dis* + *appear*, *un* + *necessary*, *mis* + *spell*. Find the real word inside and the doubling sorts itself out.
