## The mnemonic

**Too** has an extra O because it means *extra*—too much, too many, and "me too" as in *also*.

**Two** is the number, and it keeps the W from *twice*, *twin*, and *twelve*.

**To** is everything else: direction (*to the store*) and the front half of a verb (*to run*). It's the default—if the other two don't apply, it's this one.
