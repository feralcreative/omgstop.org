---
title: "Texas sharpshooter"
category: Logical fallacies
order: 124
summary: "You drew the target around the bullet holes."
rule: "Picking the pattern <em>after</em> seeing the data isn't a finding."
snark: "Now go predict it first."
examples:
  - wrong: "\"Our campaign worked—look at the three regions that grew.\""
    right: "\"What happened across all twelve?\""
    note: "Three winners out of twelve is what randomness looks like."
---

Named for the shooter who fires at a barn and paints the target afterwards. In practice it's running many comparisons and reporting the ones that landed, which guarantees a result whether or not anything is there.

**How to answer it:** ask what was predicted *in advance*, and how many things were measured. A hypothesis chosen after the data is a description, not a test.
