## The logic

Both halves describe what happens **to** the person: they come first, they get served first. Dropping the *-d* turns the second half active, which now says the first to arrive has to do the serving.

Nobody thinks that's what it means, which is precisely why the missing D survives—the phrase still communicates perfectly. It just isn't the phrase.
