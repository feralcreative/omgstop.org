## The test

Say **"you are"** in its place. Survives? **You're.** Doesn't? **Your.**

There is no third option and no edge case. This is the same test as [they're/their/there](/theyre), and it works for the same reason: one of them is a contraction and contractions can always be expanded.

Autocorrect will not save you here. Both are real words spelled correctly, so nothing gets underlined and the mistake ships.
