## Where the X comes from

The Italian **espresso** means "pressed out"—the water is forced through the grounds under pressure. It shares a root with *express* but the Italian spelling has no X.

English speakers hear the word, recognise *express*, and reasonably conclude it must be about speed. It isn't. It's about pressure.

## The caveat

*Expresso* appears in some dictionaries as a variant, and it's been used in English for a century. It is nonetheless the version that gets you a look from anyone who works with coffee.
