## Where it comes from

Say *could've* out loud. It sounds essentially identical to *could of*, and if you've mostly heard the phrase rather than read it, *of* is a completely reasonable guess at the spelling.

It's still wrong. **Of** is a preposition and cannot follow a modal verb. There's nothing for it to do in that sentence.

## The fix

Expand the contraction. *Could've* becomes *could have*. If writing *of* there felt fine, that's the habit talking—the phrase has no *of* in it and never has.
