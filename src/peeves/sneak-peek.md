## The mnemonic

**P-EE-K** has two Es, like *s-ee*. Both are about looking.

**Peak** is a summit—the peak of a mountain, peak traffic, peak season.

## Why it's everywhere

This one shows up constantly in marketing copy, where "sneak peek" is a standard phrase and the writer is moving fast. Spellcheck won't catch it because *peak* is a perfectly good word, so it ships to the whole mailing list.
