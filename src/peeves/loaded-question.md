---
title: "loaded question"
category: Logical fallacies
order: 119
summary: "There is an assumption smuggled into that question."
rule: "A question that can't be answered without conceding something isn't a <em>question</em>."
snark: "So when did you stop arguing in bad faith?"
examples:
  - wrong: "\"Why does your team keep missing deadlines?\""
    right: "\"Are we missing deadlines? Let's look.\""
    note: "Answering at all concedes the premise. Reject the premise first."
---

The classic is *have you stopped beating your wife*—yes and no both convict you. Real ones are subtler, but the structure is the same: a contested claim disguised as background to a question.

**How to answer it:** refuse the frame before answering. "That assumes X—let's check X."
