## The test

Say "they are" out loud in place of the word. If the sentence survives, it's **they're**. If it doesn't, ask whether the thing belongs to someone—that's **their**. Everything left over is **there**.

That's it. There is no fourth case, and no situation where you get to guess.

## Why nobody catches it

They sound identical, so your ear is no help and spellcheck stays quiet—every one of them is a real word, correctly spelled, just doing someone else's job. The only fix is knowing the rule, which now you do.
