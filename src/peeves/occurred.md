## The trick

**Two Cs and two Rs.** The rule: when a word ends in a stressed vowel-plus-consonant, that consonant doubles before *-ed* or *-ing*.


Compare *refer* → *referred*, *prefer* → *preferred*. But *offer* → *offered*, because the stress is on the first syllable there. Same rule, opposite result, which is why nobody trusts it.
