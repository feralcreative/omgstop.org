## The test

**Is there a thing being placed?** If yes, **lay**. If the subject is just reclining, **lie**.

You *lay* a book down. You *lie* down. *Lay* needs a direct object the way *put* does—you can't just "put."

## The part that's genuinely unfair

The past tenses overlap, which is most of why nobody gets this right:

| | Present | Past | Past participle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Place something | lay | laid | laid |
| Recline | lie | **lay** | lain |

The past tense of *lie* is *lay*. So *yesterday I lay down* is correct, and looks wrong to almost everyone.

This is not you being careless. The language did this.
