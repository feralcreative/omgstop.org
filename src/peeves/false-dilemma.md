Also called black-or-white thinking or a false dichotomy. Presenting a spectrum as a switch makes one option look inevitable, because the alternative on offer was built to be unacceptable.

**How to answer it:** name a third option out loud. If it's real, the dilemma collapses immediately. Some dilemmas are genuine—sometimes there really are only two doors—so the question is always whether the middle was ruled out or just left out.
