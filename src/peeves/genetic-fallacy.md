---
title: "genetic fallacy"
category: Logical fallacies
order: 129
summary: "You judged the claim by where it came from."
rule: "Where an idea <em>originated</em> says nothing about whether it's true."
snark: "This came off a website with swearing on it, so ignore it."
examples:
  - wrong: "\"That's from a company blog, so it's marketing.\""
    right: "\"It might be. Are the numbers wrong?\""
    note: "Biased sources produce true statements all the time. Check the claim."
---

Origin is a reason to check carefully, not a reason to conclude. A tainted source can still be right, and a trusted one can still be wrong.

**The legitimate version:** when you can't verify a claim yourself, source reliability is most of what you have, and weighting by it is sensible. The fallacy is using origin to settle a question you *could* have checked directly.
