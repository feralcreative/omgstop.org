## The word

**Moot** is old—a *moot* was an assembly for debating things. A moot point was one worth arguing.

Modern usage flipped it: a moot point is now one that's *only* worth arguing, with no practical consequence. Law schools still hold **moot courts**, where students argue cases that don't affect anyone.

**Mute** means silent, and has nothing to do with any of this. The two sound near-identical in speech, which is the entire cause.
