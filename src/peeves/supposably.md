## The annoying part

**Supposably is a real word.** It's in the dictionary. It means "capable of being supposed"—as in, it's conceivable. You could say *that's supposably true* and mean "one could suppose that," and be correct.

You have never once meant that.

What you mean is **supposedly**: allegedly, according to what people are saying. It's the far more common word, and it's the one that fits every sentence you've put *supposably* into.

So this isn't quite a made-up word. It's worse—it's a real one, deployed with total confidence, in the wrong place.
