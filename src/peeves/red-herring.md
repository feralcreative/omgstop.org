---
title: "red herring"
category: Logical fallacies
order: 117
summary: "That's a different subject, raised on purpose."
rule: "Changing the topic is not answering it."
snark: "Anyway, the real problem is email."
examples:
  - wrong: "\"Our error rate is up.\" \"Well, the competition's site was down all week.\""
    right: "\"Yes—why is our error rate up?\""
    note: "Both may be true. Only one was the question."
---

A distraction dressed as a response, usually relevant enough to seem responsive and irrelevant enough to avoid the point. It works because conversations drift and nobody wants to be the person dragging it back.

**How to answer it:** be the person dragging it back. Ask the original question again, unchanged.
