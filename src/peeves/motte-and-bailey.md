Named for a castle design: the *bailey* is the valuable, hard-to-defend courtyard; the *motte* is the fortified mound you retreat to when attacked. The move is to occupy the bailey—the strong, interesting claim—and retreat to the motte when challenged, then wander back out once the challenger leaves.

**How to answer it:** pin the claim down. Get it stated in one sentence, agree that's the claim, and hold it there.
