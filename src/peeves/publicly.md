## The trick

**Just add -ly to public.** No A.


This is a genuine irregularity. *Basic* → *basically*, *tragic* → *tragically*, *magic* → *magically*—nearly every *-ic* adjective takes *-ally*. **Publicly** is the standard exception, which is why the wrong version looks more correct than the right one.
