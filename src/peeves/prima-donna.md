## Where it comes from

Opera. The **prima donna** is the company's leading soprano—the first lady, top of the bill. Because leading sopranos acquired a certain reputation, the title slid into an insult for anyone who behaves as though the production exists to serve them.

## Why the wrong one survives

Because *pre* and *Madonna* are both things you have heard of, and the sentence still scans if you do not stop to look at it. Written down it stops scanning immediately.

There is no Madonna in it, and nothing is happening beforehand.

## In fairness

The plural is **prima donnas**. Italian would want *prime donne*, and you are not speaking Italian, so do not do that at anyone.
