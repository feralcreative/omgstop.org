## The two jobs

An apostrophe does exactly two things:

- **Possession.** Ziad's problem. The company's policy.
- **Missing letters.** Don't (do not). It's (it is). ’90s (1990s).

Making a word plural is not on the list and never has been.

## The one that trips everyone

**`its` vs `it's`.** *It's* is always "it is" or "it has"—no exceptions. The possessive is *its*, apostrophe-free, exactly like *his* and *hers*. Nobody writes *hi's*.

If you can say "it is" in its place, use **it's**. Otherwise **its**. This is the entire rule and it takes two seconds.
