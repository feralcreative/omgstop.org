---
title: "poisoning the well"
category: Logical fallacies
order: 130
summary: "You discredited them before they opened their mouth."
rule: "Pre-loading the audience against someone isn't an <em>argument</em> against what they say."
snark: "Before you read the next one, know that the author swears."
examples:
  - wrong: "\"Before Sam speaks, remember he's been wrong before.\""
    right: "\"Let's hear it and judge the proposal.\""
    note: "This is aimed at the audience, not the argument—and it lands before there's an argument."
---

A pre-emptive ad hominem. Its power is that it works *before* the claim exists, so the audience filters everything that follows through the frame you set.

**How to answer it:** name the move. "That's about Sam, not the proposal" resets it, because poisoning only works while nobody points at it.
