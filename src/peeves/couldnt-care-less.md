## The logic

The phrase claims your level of caring is at absolute zero—you *could not* care less than this, because there is no less.

Drop the *n't* and you've said the opposite: you could care less, which means you currently care at least somewhat.

## The defence, and why it's thin

Some argue the shortened version is sarcastic—*oh, I could care less*—and sarcasm inverts it back. That's plausible for the people who say it that way and generous to everyone else, who are plainly just dropping a syllable they didn't notice.

One contraction fixes it, and then nobody has to have this conversation.
