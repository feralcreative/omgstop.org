---
title: "sunk cost"
category: Logical fallacies
order: 121
summary: "You already spent it. It's gone either way."
rule: "Money and time already spent are <em>gone</em>. Only future costs and benefits count."
snark: "Throwing good money after bad is still throwing money."
examples:
  - wrong: "\"We've put eight months in, we can't stop now.\""
    right: "\"From today, is finishing worth what it'll cost?\""
    note: "The eight months are spent whether you continue or not. They're not an argument."
---

The only rational question is whether the *remaining* cost is worth the *remaining* benefit. What's already spent appears on both sides of that comparison and therefore cancels.

Knowing this doesn't stop it working. Loss aversion is wired in deep, which is why "we're too far in" gets said in rooms full of people who all know better.
